Web AppMethodology

My Web App Testing Methodology

A full-lifecycle, hunter-grade approach refined through real bug bounty programs. Starts with passive recon and cert-transparency subdomain mapping, moves through active enumeration (ffuf, Arjun, waybackurls), then deep Burp Suite proxying across every role. Testing covers the complete OWASP stack — SQLi, XSS, SSTI, XXE, IDOR, SSRF, CSRF, broken auth, JWT attacks, OAuth misconfigs, race conditions, and business-logic flaws. Every finding is chained for maximum impact, scored with CVSS v3.1, and reported with a reproducible PoC and concrete remediation. No scanner-first shortcuts — manual testing always leads.
