about me

Who I Am

I'm Darshak Patel, a B.Tech CSE (Cybersecurity) student driven by curiosity about how digital systems break — and how to make them unbreakable. I pursue hands-on learning through CTF competitions, lab environments, and open-source security research.

My work spans three high-impact domains: ethical hacking, web application pentesting, and AI/LLM security research. Understanding how attackers think is the only true path to building resilient defenses.

Actively seeking internships, bug bounty collaborations, and mentorship to grow from student to professional security practitioner.
